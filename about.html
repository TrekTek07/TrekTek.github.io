My name is Gayle Lynn, and I will be your instructor in The Buck $tarts Here Grant Writing Masterclass.

I hold a Master of Arts degree in Counseling and have built a career at the intersection of education, community development, and funding strategy. As a seasoned nationwide public speaker and Chief Executive Officer of Life Enhancers Life Coaching LLC, I bring real-world leadership and a passion for empowering others to every session.

My background spans extensive work as a counselor, program developer, and university instructor. I have served in roles as a director, manager, and consultant in grant writing and education across institutions including the California State Department of Education, the Riverside County Office of Education, and several Unified School Districts throughout California.

In this masterclass, you will learn why writing a successful grant requires understanding the funder's objectives and how to align your proposal with their goals. I will teach you how to research potential funding sources thoroughly, craft a compelling narrative that clearly outlines your project's impact and sustainability, highlight your team's expertise to build credibility, and ensure your budget is realistic and justifiable — demonstrating efficient use of funds.

"This wasn't born on the internet. It was built in the room."

This methodology has been taught live and has helped real people secure real grant funding. That experience, that track record, and that real-world knowledge is exactly what you get when you enroll.
